# Circuit profiles

Profiles of the 25 circuits in the application. Length and corner count describe the Grand Prix layout. Straights are described by their location and importance, since their number depends on how individual sections are counted.

The **downforce category** groups tracks by their typical balance between cornering support and straight-line speed. It is a working classification, not a physical measurement or a required wing setting. Surface grip and tyre wear vary with conditions and are described where the sources provide specific information.

Lengths and corner counts use [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025), the [circuit library](https://thef1family.com/circuits), and the [track catalogue](https://formula-1.website.yandexcloud.net/tracks/). Each profile also lists its own sources. Accessed 8 November 2026.

## Australia — Albert Park

**Length:** 5.278 km · **Corners:** 14 · **Downforce category:** Medium-high

**Corners and straights:** Fast and flowing, with direction changes and medium- and high-speed corners. Turn 3 is slow; after Turn 6, a fast section leads into Turns 9 and 10. The surface can initially be slippery and gain grip as cars run.

**Driving characteristics:** Fast corners call for stability and aerodynamic support, while acceleration zones reward straight-line speed. Braking and exits from slow corners affect speed on the following section.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/australia), [OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/news/f1-25-track-guides.3245/?full=1), [University of Bergamo study](https://aisberg.unibg.it/retrieve/e40f7b88-612b-afca-e053-6605fe0aeaf2/TDUnibg_Ravelli-Umberto.pdf).

## China — Shanghai

**Length:** 5.451 km · **Corners:** 16 · **Downforce category:** Medium-high

**Corners and straights:** Long, linked corners and a 1.2 km straight. The opening sequence tightens progressively, Turns 7 and 8 generate high loads, and the straight ends with braking for a slow hairpin.

**Driving characteristics:** Sustained corners demand grip and balance, while the long straight makes aerodynamic drag and braking stability important. Resurfacing in 2025 increased grip; the long corners particularly load the front-left tyre.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/china), [Formula 1 (2)](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/circuit-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-shanghai-international.1b0f0ghbsPMRsHJoHTt6eB.1b0f0ghbsPMRsHJoHTt6eB).

## Japan — Suzuka

**Length:** 5.807 km · **Corners:** 18 · **Downforce category:** Medium-high

**Corners and straights:** Fast and technical. The first-sector Esses link direction changes; Degner and 130R are fast sections, while the hairpin and final chicane reduce speed.

**Driving characteristics:** Support and stability matter through the Esses and fast turns, while full-throttle sections call for efficiency. Sustained cornering loads stress the tyres, but Brembo ranks Suzuka among the least demanding circuits for brakes.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/japan), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Bahrain — Sakhir

**Length:** 5.412 km · **Corners:** 15 · **Downforce category:** Medium

**Corners and straights:** Straights, heavy braking zones and slow corners alternate with medium- and high-speed sections. Turn 10 is particularly difficult: a tight, off-camber left entered under downhill braking; Turn 12 is fast.

**Driving characteristics:** Straights reward aerodynamic efficiency, while slow corners and their exits require stability and traction. The surface is very rough and can overheat tyres; Brembo ranks Sakhir among the most demanding circuits for brakes.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/bahrain), [McLaren](https://www.mclaren.com/racing/formula-1/2025/chinese-grand-prix/the-chinese-gp-briefing-powered-by-google-cloud/), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Saudi Arabia — Jeddah

**Length:** 6.174 km · **Corners:** 27 · **Downforce category:** Medium-low

**Corners and straights:** A very fast street circuit, with many linked corners between walls and braking zones for tighter turns. Formula 1 describes it as one of the fastest tracks on the calendar.

**Driving characteristics:** Straights and fast corners require a balance between aerodynamic drag and stability. Nearby walls leave little room for error and heavy braking requires precision; Brembo includes Jeddah among the circuits demanding on brakes.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/saudi-arabia), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Miami — Miami International Autodrome

**Length:** 5.412 km · **Corners:** 19 · **Downforce category:** Medium

**Corners and straights:** Three straights combine with corners of varied speeds. Turns 13–16 include elevation changes and a chicane with an uphill approach, crest and downhill exit; the rest of the lap includes fast sections and heavy braking.

**Driving characteristics:** Straight-line speed is balanced against control in the narrow sector and stability through direction changes. The track was resurfaced in 2023 and grip improves as the surface rubbers in during the weekend.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/miami), [Pirelli](https://cdn.connectsites.net/user_files/f4/downloads/000/075/544/Saturday%20May%203%20Miami%20GP%20Final%20pdf.pdf?1746282734=), [OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/news/f1-25-track-guides.3245/?full=1).

## Canada — Circuit Gilles-Villeneuve

**Length:** 4.361 km · **Corners:** 14 · **Downforce category:** Medium-low

**Corners and straights:** Fast and stop-start, with straights separated by chicanes and a hairpin. Braking is heavy and the final chicane runs close to the Wall of Champions.

**Driving characteristics:** Formula 1 describes it as a low-downforce circuit. Straight-line speed matters, as do braking stability, kerb handling and traction out of the hairpin.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/canada), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Monaco — Monte Carlo

**Length:** 3.337 km · **Corners:** 19 · **Downforce category:** High

**Corners and straights:** A narrow, twisty street circuit with slow corners, elevation changes and nearby barriers. It also has faster sections, such as the Swimming Pool chicane, but few long straights.

**Driving characteristics:** Turning ability and control are priorities; teams use high-downforce configurations. Low-speed traction and precision near walls are important. Brembo notes that drivers brake frequently, although not all braking events are equally intense.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/monaco), [FIA](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2025_monaco_grand_prix_-_car_presentation_submissions.pdf), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Spain — Barcelona-Catalunya

**Length:** 4.657 km · **Corners:** 14 · **Downforce category:** Medium-high

**Corners and straights:** Slow and fast corners combine with sustained turns such as Turn 3 and a long main straight. The layout tests both high-speed balance and response in slower turns.

**Driving characteristics:** Support matters in the long corners, while the straight calls for efficiency. Lateral loads and successive corners affect tyre use.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/spain), [Tracing Insights](https://tracinginsights.com/blog/742879/all-you-need-to-know-2025-spanish-grand-prix/).

## Austria — Red Bull Ring

**Length:** 4.318 km · **Corners:** 10 · **Downforce category:** Medium

**Corners and straights:** A short lap with significant elevation changes. The first half links straights and uphill right-handers; the second descends through a sequence of fast corners.

**Driving characteristics:** Straights reward top speed and fast corners require stability. The first braking zones follow closely; Brembo highlights their intensity and demands on brakes.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/austria), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Great Britain — Silverstone

**Length:** 5.891 km · **Corners:** 18 · **Downforce category:** Medium-high

**Corners and straights:** One of the fastest circuits, with direction-change sequences such as Maggots and Becketts, high-speed corners and some slower areas.

**Driving characteristics:** Fast corners require support and stability, while the straights reward efficiency. Tyres experience significant lateral loads; Brembo ranks Silverstone among the least demanding circuits for brakes.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/great-britain), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Belgium — Spa-Francorchamps

**Length:** 7.004 km · **Corners:** 19 · **Downforce category:** Medium-low

**Corners and straights:** Long and fast, with large elevation changes, long straights and high-speed corners. Eau Rouge and Raidillon link uphill direction changes; the middle sector contains fast corners and the lap ends with a slow chicane.

**Driving characteristics:** The straight-heavy sectors favour low drag, while the middle sector rewards aerodynamic support. Weather can vary across the circuit; braking and traction also matter.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/belgium), [Formula 1 (2)](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-risk-perspective-how-f1-drivers-engineers-and-strategists-tackle-the-spa-francorchamps-circuit.4TNjhNBBeh9ZD6wygCJfwo), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Hungary — Hungaroring

**Length:** 4.381 km · **Corners:** 14 · **Downforce category:** High

**Corners and straights:** Narrow and twisty, with many linked corners and few straights. Maintaining rhythm between consecutive turns is central to the lap.

**Driving characteristics:** Formula 1 notes that teams use downforce levels comparable to Monaco. Cornering grip and car response matter more than top speed on short straights.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/hungary), [Pit Trace](https://pit-trace.com/circuits/hungaroring).

## Netherlands — Zandvoort

**Length:** 4.259 km · **Corners:** 14 · **Downforce category:** High

**Corners and straights:** Fast and flowing, with elevation changes and banked corners. Hugenholtz and the final turn, Arie Luyendijk, feature banking; the lap links corners with little respite.

**Driving characteristics:** Support and stability matter through linked corners and banking. The layout subjects tyres to sustained lateral loads.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/netherlands), [Pit Trace](https://pit-trace.com/circuits/circuit-zandvoort).

## Italy — Monza

**Length:** 5.793 km · **Corners:** 11 · **Downforce category:** Low

**Corners and straights:** Very fast, with long straights, heavy braking for tight chicanes and fast corners such as Parabolica. The main straight is approximately 1.1 km long.

**Driving characteristics:** Top speed favours low-downforce configurations. Chicanes require stable braking and good exits; Brembo ranks Monza among the most demanding circuits for brakes. The surface is smooth and places relatively low energy demands on tyres.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/italy), [Mercedes](https://www.mercedesamgf1.com/news/what-are-the-tyre-options-for-the-2026-italian-gp), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes), [Pit Trace](https://pit-trace.com/circuits/monza-circuit).

## Spain — Madring, Madrid

**Length:** 5.474 km · **Corners:** 22 · **Downforce category:** High

**Corners and straights:** Fast areas, narrow corners and elevation changes lead to a technical final section. Turn 12, La Monumental, is long and banked; fast sections and a tighter area between walls follow.

**Driving characteristics:** The technical section favours support and control, while the fast areas require efficiency. Pirelli highlights the high vertical loads at La Monumental and the circuit's lateral demands; the game includes a version of the track in the 2026 pack.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/circuit-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-madring.NF7Mh3iag3w9GUPlihwJA), [Formula 1 (2)](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-tyres-will-the-teams-and-drivers-have-for-the-2026-spanish-grand-prix.2vlcVOBnZUFRCooVcqWG7n), [EA Sports](https://www.ea.com/games/f1/f1-25/news/f1-25-2026-season-edition-madring-tips-and-tricks).

## Azerbaijan — Baku

**Length:** 6.003 km · **Corners:** 20 · **Downforce category:** Low

**Corners and straights:** A very long main straight contrasts with slow corners and a narrow section beside the old city. Turn 1 follows braking from high speed.

**Driving characteristics:** Formula 1 identifies a choice between support in the twisty section and less drag on the straight. Heavy braking and slow-corner exits are important; walls punish mistakes.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/azerbaijan), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Singapore — Marina Bay

**Length:** 4.927 km · **Corners:** 19 · **Downforce category:** High

**Corners and straights:** A twisty street circuit with many corners between walls, an uneven surface and frequent braking. The 2023 revision reduced the corner count, but it remains physically demanding and challenging for traction.

**Driving characteristics:** Grip and cornering response carry more weight than top speed. Successive exits can raise rear tyre temperatures, and the uneven surface affects stability.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/singapore), [Formula 1 (2)](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/circuit-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-marina-bay-street-circuit.3d9yfiqIKoQhcMnRLESWbz.3d9yfiqIKoQhcMnRLESWbz), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## United States — Circuit of the Americas, Austin

**Length:** 5.513 km · **Corners:** 20 · **Downforce category:** Medium-high

**Corners and straights:** Varied, with a steep climb into Turn 1, fast first-sector Esses, a long straight and a later technical section. Turns 3–6 resemble the fast sequences at Silverstone and Suzuka.

**Driving characteristics:** The Esses and linked turns require support and stable direction changes; the long straight rewards top speed. Pirelli highlights lateral load, traction and tyre demands.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/united-states), [Pirelli](https://cdn.connectsites.net/user_files/f4/downloads/000/077/861/2025%20USGP%202%20Saturday%20October%2018%20Final%20pdf.pdf?1760741645=).

## Mexico — Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez

**Length:** 4.304 km · **Corners:** 17 · **Downforce category:** Medium-high

**Corners and straights:** A long main straight combines with braking zones and a technical section through the stadium. The circuit is more than 2,000 metres above sea level.

**Driving characteristics:** Thin air reduces generated downforce and affects cooling; the straight and technical sector place different demands on the car. Brembo includes Mexico City among the circuits highly demanding on brakes.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/mexico), [Formula 1 (2)](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-risk-perspective-how-f1-drivers-engineers-and-strategists-tackle-the-mexico-city-grand-prix.B8P8xr5i1sPDgQOmL7hzs), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Brazil — Interlagos

**Length:** 4.309 km · **Corners:** 15 · **Downforce category:** Medium

**Corners and straights:** Short and undulating, it begins with the Senna Esses, continues through a linked infield section and ends with a long climb to the main straight.

**Driving characteristics:** The infield requires grip through linked corners; the long climb makes acceleration and straight-line efficiency important. Williams highlights traction out of Turn 12 and locking risk at several braking zones.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/brazil), [Williams](https://www.williamsf1.com/articles/b58ad6ed-d4b8-4ca6-857d-95f26dee244c/technical-brief-sao-paulo).

## United States — Las Vegas

**Length:** 6.201 km · **Corners:** 17 · **Downforce category:** Low

**Corners and straights:** A very fast street circuit with long straights and braking zones for tight corners. Formula 1 compares its average speed with Monza's.

**Driving characteristics:** Long straights favour low aerodynamic drag; braking and acceleration from low speeds require control. Low temperatures can make tyre operation difficult.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/las-vegas), [Pirelli](https://cdn.connectsites.net/user_files/f4/downloads/000/070/211/Thursday%202023%20Las%20Vegas%20GP%20newsletter%20final%20pdf.pdf?1700269693=), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Qatar — Lusail

**Length:** 5.419 km · **Corners:** 16 · **Downforce category:** High

**Corners and straights:** Fast and flowing, dominated by medium- and high-speed corners and a main straight over a kilometre long. Linked turns keep the car under cornering load for much of the lap.

**Driving characteristics:** Cornering support and lateral stability must coexist with straight-line speed. Fast corners put high demands on tyres; Brembo notes that few braking events are at maximum intensity.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/qatar), [Brembo](https://www.brembo.com/es/motorsport/formula1/circuitos-desafiantes).

## Abu Dhabi — Yas Marina

**Length:** 5.281 km · **Corners:** 16 · **Downforce category:** Medium

**Corners and straights:** A slow hairpin leads onto a 1.2 km straight, followed by another major braking zone. The rest combines linked corners and a technical final section; the 2021 changes altered several slow turns.

**Driving characteristics:** Acceleration out of the hairpin and straight-line speed compete with the grip needed elsewhere. Some braking takes place while the car is still under lateral load.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/united-arab-emirates), [OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/news/f1-25-track-guides.3245/?full=1).

## Italy — Imola

**Length:** 4.909 km · **Corners:** 19 · **Downforce category:** Medium-high

**Corners and straights:** Fast and technical, with chicanes, corners such as Piratella and Acque Minerali, and elevation changes. Acque Minerali combines a fast entry with braking for a tight turn before the climb towards the Variante Alta chicane.

**Driving characteristics:** Fast corners require stability and support; chicanes and acceleration zones call for kerb control and traction. The combination requires a balance between agility and straight-line speed.

Sources: [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2025/emiliaromagna), [OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/news/f1-25-track-guides.3245/?full=1).
